## Signature series: Stop. Think. Assess

**Tagline:** Human factors for real divers.

**Core idea:** A calm look at dive decisions, task loading, human factors and the small moments that shape safe diving.

**House rule:** Critique the decision pathway, not the person.

### Framework

| Step | Question |
|---|---|
| **STOP** | Something has changed. |
| **THINK** | What do we know, and what are we assuming? |
| **ASSESS** | What are the risks, options and easiest safe next step? |
| **DECIDE** | Continue, change the plan, regroup, ascend or call the dive. |
| **REVIEW** | What can we learn without blame? |

### Series style

- Blame-free, positive and practical.
- Separate known facts from assumptions.
- Look for the pause point.
- Identify 1–3 human factors in play, rather than trying to explain everything.
- End with a useful takeaway for ordinary divers.
- Works as text article, talking-head video, podcast segment, or learning-review/reaction format.

- **Gear Reviews & Guides** — Sponsor-relevant but honest kit content, guides and tier lists.
- **Fitness for Divers** — Strength, mobility, endurance and practical fitness for all ages/body types.
- **Mini Lectures** — Educational content that converts cleanly into talking-head videos/podcasts.
- **Marine Life / Reef** — Aquarium keeper perspective, reef health, fish ID and observation.
- **Tools / Templates** — Downloadable checklists, dive log templates, iPhone Shortcuts and gas calculators.
- **Tier Lists** — Clickable buyer-help content with clear criteria and no rage-bait.

## Sponsor-readiness principles

| Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Trust first | Reader value before monetisation. |
| Real-world use | Field notes from real UK/training dives beat spec-sheet hype. |
| Clear disclosure | If gear is loaned or sponsored later, disclose plainly. |
| Who it suits / who should skip | Honest context-specific recommendations. |
| Guides over hype | Teach setup and decision-making, not just product enthusiasm. |
| Data where possible | Use logs, conditions, photos and repeated use before strong conclusions. |
