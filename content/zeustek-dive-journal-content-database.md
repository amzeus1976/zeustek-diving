## ZD-006 Deep Diver personalisation notes

- **Title:** Deep Diver: More Than Just a Bigger Number
- **Category:** Training Notes; Human Factors; Technical Diving
- **Pillar:** Training / Human Factors / Technical Pathway
- **Reader Value:** Depth as gas reality, temperature change, narcosis humility, redundancy and the start of technical-thinking.
- **Sponsor Value:** Shows mature review of training, gas planning, redundancy, sidemount interest and safety culture awareness.
- **Tags CSV:** Deep Diver,PADI Deep Diver,Pyramids Divers,Red Sea Diving,Sharm Diving,Narcosis,Gas Planning,Pony Bottle,Sidemount,Technical Diving,Safety Culture,Dive Medicals,Human Factors,Dive Safety,Diver Development,ZeusTek Diving
- **Notes:** Personal details: course done over four days with Pyramids Divers alongside Night Diver. Instructor was very good, but centre safety culture raised concerns after hearing comments that sounded like new/try divers were being encouraged to fudge medicals; mention carefully as personal concern and that it was reported. Deep dive: no obvious narcosis at 40 m and instructor saw no task-performance difference, but do not frame as immunity. Greedy breather: gas needle visibly dropped at 40 m; reassurance from side-slung pony and 38% mix at 5 m safety stop. Noticed chill from 20-30 m down and definitely at 40 m even in Red Sea wearing 3 mm shortie. Pony bottle experience helped spark sidemount-over-twinset interest. Gas planning and what-if planning started deeper interest in deco/tec for wreck penetration, longer dives and added safety margins.
